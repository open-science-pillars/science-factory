# Reading the reviews

The three registers in this folder are history: records of what reviewers found and what was done about it. They are kept as they were written and are not brought level with the repository. Two things have changed around them since, and each register says so in one line at its top.

**The records they cite are no longer on `main`.** The practice runs, the study and exploration logs, the receipts, the drafted pages and both runs of the demo were taken out of this repository in November 2026, when it became the factory only. They are at the branch `records-2026-11`, with the tools as they stood then. A number a register cites from one of those records is historical: check it there. Where a number can be made again today from this repository alone, README.md, under "Try it", has the command.

**The paths are as they were.** The registers were written before 2026-11-26, when the files that sat together in one folder, `mvp/`, were moved into the layout the README shows, and they name files by the paths of the day they were written. To follow a path in them: `mvp/sf.py` is now `tools/sf.py`; `mvp/loops/explore.py`, `mvp/loops/knowledge.py` and `mvp/loops/wiki_server.py` are in `tools/`; `mvp/procedure.yaml`, `mvp/change-log.jsonl` and `mvp/templates/` are in `factory/`; `mvp/gain.py`, `mvp/confront.py`, `mvp/test_cases.py`, `mvp/known_answer_tests.py` and `mvp/system_trial.py` are in `methods/gain/`; `mvp/compute/` is `compute/`; `assistants/check_roles.py` is `tools/check_roles.py`; and the documents that stood at the top of the repository (the design, the plan, the word list, the two ways of working, how to create a factory) are in `docs/`. Anything under `mvp/studies/`, `mvp/exhibits/`, `studies/` or `examples/baton-rouge/` other than its page and its two requests is at `records-2026-11` and not here. Two files a later register names were removed with the records, because they existed only to keep old records checkable: the table of moved files and `tools/check_old_records.py`.
