# Request

*One page, in your own words and your own units. The design is that the analysis assistant (an AI agent) turns this into an analysis plan and that you are shown the plan before anything is locked. Leave a line empty if you do not know. For the five lines marked "asked back" the assistant asks you, four questions in all, and then stops; any other line may stay empty.*

*Where this stands today: the page and the questions back are a convention. The study tool binds the finished request to the study by checksum (`sf.py start --request`), so it cannot change unnoticed after the lock, and it checks nothing inside it yet.*

**What I want to know.** One to three sentences, as you would say it to a colleague. A suspicion is fine. "No hypothesis yet, just look" is an honest answer too. It asks for an exploratory study, which is designed and not built yet: today such a request can be written down and taken no further.

**The thing.** The product, record, budget, model or place this is about, with versions if you know them.

**Why it matters.** The decision or the paper this feeds.

**The difference that matters, and where it comes from** (asked back). The number that would change what you do. For example "within 10 cm, from our release rule" or "a residual under the combined uncertainty". If nobody has stated one, say so.

**What I have already looked at** (asked back). Years, places, products and versions you or your group have plotted or fitted. All of it becomes working data.

**What could be held out.** Anything not yet opened: a later period, sites or floats or basins you have not touched, a product you have never loaded, a release not yet out, forecasts that could be issued now and scored later.

**What I will do with each answer** (asked back).
- If it holds:
- If it does not:
- If it cannot tell:

**By when** (asked back). A date, or none.

**How I expect it to be fooled.** The one thing about this data that trips people up. It becomes a hard case the method must survive in the known-answer tests.

**Who is on it** (asked back). Authors, and who should approve the plan with the steward at the lock. The tool records one person at the lock today, the steward; your own approval there is designed and not built.

**What I want back.** A page, a plot, fields as files, a script to rerun, a memo for a manager.

**Anyone else's product involved?** If the answer says something about a named provider's product, the result is delivered to you privately at once and to them before any public reference.
